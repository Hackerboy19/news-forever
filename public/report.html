Priority actions

  1. HighRefresh dated keywords. The dominant terms are 2023 editions; updating metadata of evergreen pages to 2026 terms ("miss india 2026", "mrs india 2026 registration") captures current search volume. Editable per article from the new admin panel.
  2. HighFill the 76 missing alt texts. Image search is a natural traffic source for pageant coverage; each missing alt_tag is a lost image-search entry. One-line edit per article in the panel.
  3. MediumTrim meta descriptions to 120–165 characters. Average today is 183 — Google truncates them; only 31% sit in the ideal band that maximises click-through.
  4. MediumGenerate sitemap.xml + robots.txt at domain cutover. When the new build takes over newsforever.in, submit the sitemap in Search Console the same day.
  5. MediumPlan server-side rendering. Makes every crawler and social scraper see meta tags instantly; structural upgrade, scoped separately.
